# 62: Delete and expire inactive conversations in History

**Status:** ready-for-human  
**Category:** bug  
**Priority:** P2  
**Reference:** [History rows without Delete controls](../references/history-inactive-delete-gap.png)  
**Related:** [12: Conversation desk](12-messenger-style-conversation-desk.md), [47: 30-day transcript retention](47-compact-history-and-thirty-day-default.md), [51: Delete and clear history](51-delete-and-clear-history.md)

## Agent Brief

**Summary:** Every conversation shown in History should be eligible for its confirmed manual deletion and the configured transcript-retention period, regardless of whether its stored state says `closed`, `claimed`, `awaiting_staff`, or `bot_active`.

**Current behavior:** History contains closed conversations and conversations that moved there after 24 hours without a visitor or staff message. Only `closed` rows show Delete. Owner Clear history and the retention sweep also purge only `closed` rows. An inactive `claimed`, `awaiting_staff`, or `bot_active` conversation has no dashboard path to close it and can remain in History indefinitely. The screenshot shows these rows without Delete controls.

**Desired behavior:** Use current History eligibility as the boundary for deletion. A staff member may delete one History conversation after the existing confirmation dialog; an owner may clear all History conversations after a dialog showing the actual count. The existing configurable retention period (30 days by default) also expires inactive History conversations 30 days after they *entered* History: last meaningful activity plus the 24-hour inactivity window, then the retention period. Closed conversations continue to expire from their closure time. No live conversation is deleted by either manual action or retention. Do not change a quiet conversation's stored state to `closed` solely to make it deletable: staff closure and inactivity mean different things.

**Key interfaces:**

- The History predicate must be shared conceptually by row controls, manual deletion, bulk clearing, and retention. Recheck eligibility when deletion occurs, not only when the list was rendered.
- The transactional store purge must remove the conversation, transcript, visitor sessions, return links, visitor contact details, and notification rows together; audit records retain their existing detached behavior. A failed eligibility check must leave all of those records untouched in both SQLite and Postgres.
- Keep the existing staff/owner permissions and confirmation dialog pattern from ticket 51.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [x] A quiet `claimed`, `awaiting_staff`, or `bot_active` row in History shows the same per-row Delete action as a closed row. A live row in Conversations does not.
- [x] Any staff role can delete one History row after confirming; the dialog states that removal is permanent and identifies the conversation. Cancel, Escape, and backdrop dismissal remove nothing.
- [x] Owner Clear history includes **all** History rows in its displayed count and deletion, including quiet non-closed rows. Non-owners cannot invoke it. The button is absent or disabled when History is empty.
- [x] The APIs reject deletion of a conversation that is still live or became live since the list loaded, with a clear conflict response. In that case no related data is removed. Unknown ids remain 404.
- [x] The configured transcript-retention period applies to quiet non-closed History rows from the instant they crossed the 24-hour inactivity boundary. Closed rows still use `closedAt`. The default remains 30 days and the owner override still wins. An active conversation of any age survives.
- [x] Manual and automatic purges produce the same fresh-chat behavior for a visitor with a stale session or return link. Audit records remain, with conversation links detached as before.
- [x] The History confirmation wording and retention settings explain that *all* History conversations, including inactive ones, can be removed; they do not imply that only closed chats are affected.
- [x] Tests cover all four stored states, the exact inactivity and retention boundaries, a conversation becoming active between display/listing and purge, owner/staff permissions, and transactional no-partial-delete behavior for both stores.

**Out of scope:** Reopening or replying to inactive conversations; changing the 24-hour inactivity window; changing the retention period or analytics retention; deleting live conversations; redesigning the History layout.

## Comments

2026-11-06: Filed from the user's screenshot and request to make old chats removable. Code-path verification: History selects conversations that are no longer active; the row, both deletion APIs, and the retention sweep currently restrict deletion to `closed`. A quiet non-closed row therefore has no Delete control and no automatic expiry. Recommended policy is to make History membership the deletion boundary, with an eligibility check inside the store's atomic purge so a conversation that becomes active during a request is preserved.

2026-11-07: Implemented (`Status:` → ready-for-human). All eight boxes checked; the eighth carries one caveat (Postgres execution, below).

- **One History rule.** `isConversationActive` (closed, or last meaningful activity more than 24 h ago ⇒ History) stays the single definition. `historyQuietBefore(now)` expresses it as the ISO cutoff the stores recheck, and `historyEnteredAt` names the "entered History" instant (closure, or lastActivity + 24 h). Row controls, the delete route, Clear history and the retention sweep all go through it.
- **Store.** `purgeClosedConversation(id)` became `purgeHistoryConversation(id, quietBefore)` and `listConversationsClosedBefore` became `listExpiredHistoryConversations(closedBefore, quietBefore)` on both stores. The purge checks History membership **before** touching anything. SQLite: check, then ROLLBACK on refusal. Postgres: `SELECT … FOR UPDATE`, refuse before any write, and a backstop that throws to roll back.
  - **Fixed a latent Postgres bug:** the old purge ran every child DELETE first, then returned `false` from `sql.begin` when the state check failed. That COMMITS, so a refused purge on Postgres had silently deleted the transcript, sessions and return links. SQLite rolled back correctly.
  - Audit rows are still detached, not deleted.
- **Routes.** A single delete is open to any staff role. A live conversation gets **409 `conversation_live`** ("still live on the Conversations tab…"), and one that turned live after the list loaded is refused by the store with all related data intact. Unknown ids stay 404. Clear history stays owner-only and now enumerates every History row (same rule), with each purge rechecking.
- **Retention.** A quiet row expires once `lastActivity < now − period − 24 h`, i.e. strictly past the period measured from entering History. Closed rows still read `closedAt`. The 30-day default and the owner override are unchanged; a live conversation of any age survives.
- **UI and wording.** Delete shows on every History row (`deletable = !item.active`) and never on live rows. Clear history counts `history.length` and hides when History is empty. The Delete and Clear dialogs, the retention form, and the settings summary ("30 days in History") now say *closed or inactive*.
