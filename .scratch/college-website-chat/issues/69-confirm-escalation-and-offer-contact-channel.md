# 69: Confirm escalation and offer Chat, Email, or Phone contact

**Status:** ready-for-human  
**Category:** enhancement  
**Priority:** P2  
**Related:** [06: Typed handoff phrases](06-ambiguous-and-handoff-phrases.md), [37: Visitor-consented human help](37-unknown-offer-human-before-escalating.md), [53: Visitor emails](53-visitor-emails-page.md), [66: Required pre-chat contact](66-require-visitor-contact-before-chat-and-relabel-status-colors.md), [67: Click-to-handoff options](67-clickable-suggestions-that-request-staff-handoff.md), [70: Branding settings](70-configurable-chatbot-and-dashboard-branding.md), [project spec](../spec.md)

## Current behavior

Every new chat requires Full name, Email, and Phone before the visitor can ask anything. Those values are stored on the conversation, the name labels the chat, and every conversation with an email immediately appears in **Visitor emails**. A typed Handoff phrase or clicked Handoff Option escalates immediately. An unknown question instead offers **Chat with a human**, and that click starts the existing waiting-for-staff flow.

The dashboard has transient red **New message** attention and green **Needs human** waiting status, but no persistent staff task for an email or phone follow-up. Merely opening a conversation clears New message attention.

## Desired behavior

Treat the required Full name, Email, and Phone as **temporary contact details** when the initial form is accepted. Keep them on the conversation through normal transcript retention and continue using the name as the conversation label, but do not list the conversation as a saved contact until the visitor creates a contact request.

When a visitor clicks a configured Handoff Option or types a configured Handoff phrase, record their wording and ask:

> **Would you like a staff member to join this chat?**

Offer two descriptive actions: **Yes, chat with staff** and **No, contact me another way**. The prompt and its available actions must survive a reload and must not cause any alert, saved-contact entry, or handoff until the visitor chooses.

### Yes: Chat

Yes creates one contact request with medium **Chat**, promotes all three temporary fields into the saved contact list, and enters the existing waiting-for-staff flow. The widget pauses input as it does today, the conversation appears as green Needs human/Waiting, the configured staff email alert and notification sound occur once, and authorized staff can Take over. A successful Take over completes the open Chat request.

The existing **Chat with a human** action after an unknown question stays a one-click action rather than showing a second confirmation. That explicit click also creates a Chat contact request, saves all three fields, alerts once, and enters waiting.

### No: Email or Phone

No presents **Email** and **Phone** choices. Selecting one creates a contact request with that medium, promotes all three temporary fields into the saved contact list, sends the configured staff email alert and notification sound once, and leaves the conversation bot-active. The bot confirms the selected medium with a masked version of the saved destination and truthful, support-hours-aware wording.

An open Email or Phone request adds a persistent red **Contact Me** status to the Active conversation. It does not clear merely because staff view the transcript. Owner and staff roles receive a **Mark contacted** action; using it completes the request without adding a chatbot message or closing the conversation. A separate transient **New message** indicator can appear and clear alongside Contact Me without replacing it.

Only one contact request may be open per conversation. While one is open, hide the escalation/contact actions and reject duplicate direct requests. Once staff completes it, the visitor may create another request. Keep one saved-contact row per conversation showing the latest medium; preserve earlier request events in the conversation's request/audit history.

Rename **Visitor emails** to **Visitor contacts** in the sidebar and page. Include only conversations that have created at least one contact request. Each row shows the visitor's name, email, phone, latest Contact medium, and the chat-start time (the time the initial form was accepted). Contact requests and promoted contact records follow the conversation's existing retention and deletion lifecycle.

## Decisions confirmed by the user

- Keep Full name, Email, and Phone as the initial temporary fields.
- The chat-start timestamp is when the initial form is accepted.
- Prompt only clicked Handoff Options and typed Handoff phrases; do not add a second prompt after the existing unknown-question human action.
- Use descriptive prompt buttons rather than bare Yes/No labels.
- Both Yes and a selected Email/Phone follow-up promote all three fields into the saved contact list and record Contact medium.
- Chat uses the normal waiting-for-staff behavior. Only Email and Phone keep the bot active and use red Contact Me.
- Taking over completes Chat; Mark contacted completes Email or Phone. Owner and staff may mark requests contacted.
- Alert through dashboard status, configured staff email, and notification sound once.
- Allow one open request, then permit a later request after completion. Show the latest medium in one row per conversation.
- Rename the page to Visitor contacts and show all details, latest medium, and chat-start time; do not add a visitor-facing message when staff completes an external contact.
- Keep unpromoted temporary details with the transcript until normal retention, while excluding them from Visitor contacts.
- Configurable college-owned email/phone settings are not part of this request.

## Out of scope

Automated outbound email, SMS, or phone calls; verifying that external contact actually occurred; department-specific assignment; changing ordinary answer matching; changing the required initial form; and the separate branding work in ticket 70.
