# 73: Hide empty clear successors from Active

**Status:** ready-for-human  
**Category:** bug  
**Priority:** P2  
**Related:** [14: Clear visitor conversation and keep staff history](14-clear-visitor-conversation-keep-staff-history.md), [47: Compact History and thirty-day default](47-compact-history-and-thirty-day-default.md), [48: History tab links to dead route](48-history-tab-links-to-dead-route.md), [62: Delete and expire all History conversations](62-delete-and-expire-all-history-conversations.md), [65: Preserve conversation-list scroll while browsing](65-preserve-conversation-list-scroll-while-browsing.md), [66: Require visitor contact before chat and relabel status colors](66-require-visitor-contact-before-chat-and-relabel-status-colors.md)

## Current behavior

When a visitor selects **Clear conversation**, the old conversation is correctly closed, retained in History, and linked to one newly created successor session. The successor is immediately classified as active even though it has no visitor-authored message. Repeated clears therefore leave staff-facing rows labeled `Visitor · bot` with a `No messages` preview under Active.

Opening one of these blank successor rows selects an active conversation with no transcript, producing an apparently empty conversation canvas. The useful cleared transcript is available under History, but the blank row does not take staff there.

### Reproduction shown in the supplied screenshots

1. Start a visitor conversation and use **Clear conversation** in the chatbot.
2. Open the staff Conversations desk.
3. Observe a new `Visitor · bot` row under Active with `No messages`, even though the cleared transcript moved to History.
4. Select that row and observe an empty transcript pane instead of the cleared conversation's History transcript.

## Desired behavior

Rename only the first desk tab from **Conversations** to **Active** and keep the second tab named **History**. Keep the dashboard page heading and sidebar destination named **Conversations**. Do not add a third tab or a nested Active/Inactive tab set: History remains the single archive for both explicitly closed and automatically inactive conversations.

Keep Clear conversation's existing transaction and visitor behavior unchanged: close the old conversation, retain its staff transcript, revoke its visitor credential and unused return links, create exactly one fresh successor session, and preserve the `replaced_by` link used for retry safety.

Exclude every conversation without a visitor-authored message from the staff Active and Incoming lists. This includes a fresh successor created by Clear and a session where the visitor submitted the required contact form but has not asked a question. The session remains valid on the visitor side and becomes visible to staff normally as soon as its first visitor message is stored.

If staff opens a direct URL for an empty successor created by Clear, resolve its predecessor through the existing reverse `replaced_by` relationship and redirect to the predecessor conversation route. The predecessor is closed, so the existing route selection must render it with History selected and its retained transcript visible. Empty sessions without a predecessor remain hidden from staff lists but otherwise keep their current visitor-side and direct-route behavior.

Add a store-level predecessor lookup with equivalent SQLite and Postgres implementations. Use the existing relationship; do not add or change database columns. The staff conversations API response shape remains unchanged—empty conversations are omitted server-side rather than represented with a new public field.

Do not change analytics counting, successor creation, visitor credentials, the contact gate, retention and deletion eligibility, inactivity timing, notification behavior, polling cadence, list-scroll memory, or conversation-state rules.

## Decisions confirmed by the user

- Use the existing two-tab structure rather than adding another tab layer.
- Rename the first tab to **Active** and keep **History** as the archive label.
- Hide fresh successors until their first visitor-authored message.
- Redirect a direct link for an empty clear-created successor to its predecessor's History transcript.
- Keep empty sessions without a predecessor hidden while preserving their existing visitor-side behavior.

## Out of scope
