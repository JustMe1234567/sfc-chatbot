## Current behavior

Fresh and ongoing suggested-question buttons come from published answer rules and send their question through the normal matcher. Configured **Handoff phrases** are text triggers: typing a matching phrase escalates immediately, even if an answer rule matches. Unknown questions offer a separate **Chat with a human** button. Handoff already creates one waiting conversation in the shared inbox, records a staff alert, and lets staff take over. The current bot handoff reply says it could not answer from approved topics, which would be untrue for a visitor deliberately choosing a staff option.

## Proposed behavior

Seed the three pictured phrases as **click-to-handoff options**, not as ordinary answer rules: **I want to speak to someone in admissions**, **I have a question about course prices**, and **I have a question about financial aid**. Owners/editors can add, edit, and remove further click-to-handoff options in the Answers admin area, separately from the existing typed Handoff phrases. A clicked option records its label as the visitor's message, gives a short acknowledgment, and uses the existing escalation path exactly once: waiting state, dashboard Incoming/attention indicator, configured staff email alert recipients, and staff Take over. It must not answer from a published rule on that click, even if a rule covers the same topic. Typing the same wording follows normal typed-question matching unless that wording was **separately** configured as a typed Handoff phrase. The existing waiting-state composer lock and support-hours expectations still apply.

The visitor wants an acknowledgment in the spirit of “admissions is currently busy and will be with you shortly.” Use a truthful topic-aware alternative, for example: **“Thanks for asking about admissions. I’ve notified our team, and someone will reply here as soon as they’re available.”** For other options, substitute the selected topic. If support hours are configured and staff are off duty, use the existing schedule and say staff will reply when the desk is open. Do not assert that a department is busy or promise “shortly” without that information. Do not reuse the existing “I can’t answer from my approved topics” prefix for a deliberate handoff. Ticket 66 removes visitor return-link emails, so new copy must not promise an emailed way back once that ticket is implemented.

Show these options on the fresh chat and again alongside ongoing suggestions after bot answers. They disappear while the visitor is typing a nonblank question and while a send/reply is pending, then return when the input is cleared or the bot has answered. Once human help is requested or claimed, they are hidden; there is no second handoff to request. If ticket 66's required contact gate has landed, options appear only after its form is completed.

## Decisions confirmed by the user

- All three pictured phrases should be defaults. They are handoff options rather than ordinary answer rules because clicking them must notify staff and pause bot answers.
- Owners/editors can configure additional clickable options in Answers.
- Clicks use the existing shared inbox and configured staff alert recipients.
- Only clicking a configured option triggers its special handoff action. Typed wording keeps the normal typed-question path.
