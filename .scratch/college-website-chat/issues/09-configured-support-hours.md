# 09: Display configured support hours

**What to build:** The owner can set support hours, and the visitor sees accurate handoff wording based on those hours. When hours are not configured, the widget gives a general follow-up message without promising a deadline.

**Blocked by:** 02: Unknown question reaches the shared inbox.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] An owner can set and change support hours and their time zone in the dashboard.
- [ ] The widget shows the configured hours and appropriate handoff wording inside and outside those hours.
- [ ] With no configured hours, the widget shows a general follow-up message and no specific response-time promise.
- [ ] The behavior is verified across a day boundary and the configured time zone.
