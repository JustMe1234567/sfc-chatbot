# 66: Require visitor contact before chat and show names with revised status colors

**Status:** ready-for-human  
**Category:** enhancement  
**Priority:** P2  
**Blocked by:** [62: Expire all History conversations](62-delete-and-expire-all-history-conversations.md)  
**Related:** [07: Visitor email and return](07-visitor-email-secure-return.md), [41: Welcome message](41-welcome-message-on-chat-open.md), [44: Chat status colors](44-chat-status-colors-and-sound-notifications.md), [53: Visitor emails](53-visitor-emails-page.md), [project spec](../spec.md)

## Proposed behavior

Show a brief, accessible contact form as the widget's first usable step for every **new** chat. Require full name, email address, and phone number before topic buttons, typed questions, bot answers, or human handoff can be used. After a successful save, show the current welcome message and normal chat. A returning visitor with a completed, still-valid conversation can resume without repeating the form; starting another conversation requires the form again. The server must enforce the gate, including direct API requests and legacy sessions without contact details, so hiding controls alone cannot bypass it. A failed submission keeps the entered values and shows a clear error; retrying must not create duplicate conversations. Keep the form copy short rather than adding a long data-use explanation.

Store the three fields with the conversation and validate them on the server. Use the visitor's full name as the primary label in Conversations and History, with the existing state beside it, such as **Jane Doe · claimed**, **Jane Doe · bot**, **Jane Doe · waiting**, or **Jane Doe · closed**. Keep the time and latest-message preview. Older conversations without a name use a clear fallback such as **Visitor · bot**; staff can still read them. The existing authorized conversation pane and Visitor emails page may continue to show the email. Collect and retain the phone number with the conversation, but leave any phone-specific display, call, SMS, or follow-up workflow to the separate ticket the user mentioned. Contact fields are removed with the conversation under transcript retention and manual deletion and must not enter analytics or notification-sound metadata.

Change the dashboard's status palette while preserving text/icon labels and notification sounds: **waiting for staff (`awaiting_staff`) is green**; **active bot or claimed chat is yellow**; **new unread visitor activity is red**; History remains neutral. Apply the mapping consistently to list rows, badges/counts, and the selected conversation header, with readable contrast and no color-only meaning. Do not change the underlying conversation state machine or the meaning of Active as the 24-hour recent-work list.

The currently approved [project spec](../spec.md) explicitly allows anonymous questions, optional email at handoff, and secure return-link emails. This request supersedes those flows. Remove the optional handoff-email prompt and stop sending **new** visitor return-link emails after staff replies, even though email is required at chat start; previously issued links may live until their normal expiry. Implementation must update the spec, glossary, widget wording, Visitor emails page copy, and relevant tests to state the new requirement accurately. The existing 30-day transcript-retention policy must cover the new personal details, including inactive History chats; ticket 62 is a prerequisite so that promise is true.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A fresh widget opens with clearly labeled required Full name, Email, and Phone fields and brief copy. Until valid details are accepted, no topic button or question submission can produce an answer or escalation; closing the form does not bypass the gate.
- [ ] Server-side validation rejects blank/invalid or excessively long values and prevents messages or topic-choice submissions for conversations without accepted details. Successful submission starts or unlocks exactly one conversation; pending and failure feedback is visible and retry-safe.
- [ ] A valid existing conversation resumes without asking again. Every newly started conversation, including one after Clear chat or a lapsed/closed chat, requires the form again. Existing historical conversations remain readable with a fallback name.
- [ ] The name plus state appears in the desk list and selected pane; the latest-message preview and time remain readable at desktop and phone widths. Email retains its existing authorized staff visibility. Phone is stored with the conversation but gains no phone-specific action or workflow in this ticket; unauthenticated staff surfaces never expose the fields.
- [ ] Remove the optional handoff-email request and its saved/declined copy. Do not send new visitor return-link emails after staff replies; staff escalation alerts continue as before. Previously issued links remain usable until their normal expiry. The Visitor emails page accurately describes required email capture on every new chat.
- [ ] Waiting is green, active bot/claimed conversations are yellow, and new-message alerts are red across the live desk. Each retains text or an accessible name, sufficient contrast, and existing sound behavior. History rows retain a neutral treatment.
- [ ] Transcript retention, manual delete, and Clear history remove all three fields with the conversation; no name/email/phone is copied into analytics or notification-sound metadata. Tests cover both SQLite and Postgres, direct API bypass attempts, legacy data, and the widget's blocked and successful paths.

## Decisions confirmed by the user

- Require full name, email, and phone for **every new chat**, including another chat started by a returning visitor. A still-valid existing chat resumes without re-entry.
- Show **Name · state** as the staff list's main label.
- Waiting means `awaiting_staff` and is green; active covers bot and claimed chats and is yellow; New message is red.
- Do **not** send visitor return-link emails. The required email is still captured, but the handoff flow must not ask for it again.
- Keep the initial form brief. Phone use belongs to another ticket; this one only collects and retains it with the conversation.

## Out of scope

Phone-specific display or follow-up behavior, SMS, automatic phone calls, verification codes, marketing use of contact details, changes to chat inactivity timing, and a dashboard redesign beyond the requested labels and colors.
