## Agent Brief

**Summary:** Make the Conversations/History desk resemble the supplied image: a bounded conversation workspace with an internally scrolling message list, and a page title aligned to the same centered width as the desk.

**Current behavior:** The desk is centered with a maximum width, but the `Conversations` page title is in the full-width dashboard heading outside that container. The selected conversation pane has a minimum height but no effective maximum height. A long transcript makes the pane grow to the full conversation length, pushing the reply area and other content far down the page.

**Desired behavior:** At desktop widths, the conversation list and selected conversation pane form one compact workspace below a `Conversations` heading whose left and right boundaries match the desk's content container. The list and message log scroll independently inside their own regions; the selected conversation header, status/quiet notice, and applicable reply controls remain visible rather than moving with the full transcript. The screenshot is the visual reference for proportions, spacing, and the bordered message region. On narrow screens, preserve the existing list-to-detail navigation and provide enough space to read and scroll messages without trapping the page or clipping controls.

- The existing desk components, transcript, and reply/close controls keep their current behavior and permissions. Layout may change, but a History conversation must remain read-only.
- Keep the dashboard's existing fixed sidebar and right-side content scrolling behavior at desktop sizes.
